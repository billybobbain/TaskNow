TaskNow Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

TaskNow is a task-planning app for Android. This page explains what data the app accesses and what happens to it.

Data storage

All tasks, subtasks, categories, and saved locations you create in TaskNow are stored locally on your device in a private app database. TaskNow does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not transmit your task data anywhere.

Location permission

TaskNow requests location access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) only if you choose to use location-based features, such as tagging a task with a saved location or filtering tasks by proximity to where you are. Distance calculations happen entirely on your device. Location permission is optional and only requested when you use these features.

Maps

When you use the map picker to select a location, TaskNow displays map tiles using OpenStreetMap via the OSMDroid library. Loading a map tile sends a standard network request (including your device's IP address and the map area you're viewing) to OpenStreetMap's tile servers, the same way any map or browser would. This is not linked to any TaskNow account, because none exists. See OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy for how they handle tile requests.

Device backup

TaskNow does not disable Android's standard app backup. If you have Android backup enabled on your device, your TaskNow data may be included in your device's encrypted backup to your own Google Account, the same as most other apps. This is controlled by your device's Android settings, not by TaskNow.

What TaskNow does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No account creation, sign-in, or user profiles
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties

Children's privacy

TaskNow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
